---
name: Receipts NJ sales tax
description: Receipts apply New Jersey sales tax 6.625%, per-line taxable toggle
type: feature
---
Receipts default to NJ sales tax 6.625% (editable per receipt). Each line item can be taxable or not (deposits default non-taxable). Manual receipts allowed without a reservation, with vehicle name and date.
Do not display a receipt date or time stamp at the top of the receipt or on its printed copy; keep rental-period dates.
