---
name: Returning renter verification
description: Recent verified renters should not upload ID and selfie again when signing a new reservation
type: constraint
---
Returning renters with approved license and selfie from an active rental or a rental ending within the past 90 days can reuse those documents when signing a new agreement. Require fresh photos for expired or missing license details, missing or unapproved photos, flagged/banned/blacklisted/inactive renters, or rentals outside the window. They must still sign the new agreement and complete the normal payment/review process. Allow them to choose new photos if their license changed.
