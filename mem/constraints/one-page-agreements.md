---
name: One-page rental agreements
description: All rental agreements must include every detail and signature line on one printed page
type: constraint
---
All rental agreements must fit on one page, including all terms, rental details, extensions, and the signature line. Do not remove content to achieve the fit.
This applies retroactively to older saved/uploaded agreements and moving forward to every print/download entry point, including bulk walk-in uploads, violation packets, and active reservations. Preserve original signed content; fitting must not recreate or alter signatures. Rent-to-own retains its separate two-page agreement/checklist layout.
Violation printing: the cover letter and signed rental agreement each have their own single page, not combined onto one page.
