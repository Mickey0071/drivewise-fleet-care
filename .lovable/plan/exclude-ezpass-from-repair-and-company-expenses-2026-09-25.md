# Exclude EZPass from repair and company expenses

## Changes
- Remove violation and EZPass records from the shared vehicle expense calculation.
- This removes them from vehicle repair-history expense lists, repair-history CSV/PDF downloads, vehicle totals, and company P&L totals.
- Keep violations visible only in the dedicated Violations area.

## Verification
- Confirm repair history contains only repairs, maintenance, and genuine operating expenses.
- Confirm the app builds without errors.
